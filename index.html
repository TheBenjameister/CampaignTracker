The two leading alliances fight for the central hex. The third may intervene as a disruptor under terms agreed by the players.

I

Challenge

Choose an adjacent territory. Occupied territory must be defended by a player from its controlling alliance; allied players cannot oppose one another.

II

Battle

Use that territory's mission card. The victor claims a neutral hex or takes an enemy hex; the defender retains it otherwise.

III

Requisition

Every controlled territory generates RP at the start of the week. The alliance shares its treasury and decides how it is spent.

IV

Conquest

The two alliances with the most territories enter the finale. Ties require Battle Honours, then the Warmaster's ruling.
